    • Scottish Shortbread

    Ingredients:







    Instructions:



    1. Preheat oven to 300°F. Spray an 8x8 or 9x9 baking pan with cooking spray.


    2. In a standmixer or in a larger bowl, cream the butter until fluffy. Add 1 cup of sugar gradually and mix until fluffy. Add flour and knead until a dough forms. If you have a crumbly dough, keep mixing until it comes together.


    3. Press the dough into the prepared pan and make sure it is evenly distributed. Stab the surface with a fork to create what should look like a grid. Dust the top with the remaining sugar.


    4. Cook at 300°F for 30 minutes, or until golden brown edges. Let cool for 15-20 minutes before removing from the pan.
