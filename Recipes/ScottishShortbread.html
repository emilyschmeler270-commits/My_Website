Scottish Shortbread


There are many variations of this classic recipe, but the 3 basic ingredients remain the same. This is a great recipe for beginners who are just starting out in the kitchen due to its simplicity. This recipe is also great for decorating dishes with by crumbling the shortbread, or on its own. I especially like it when paired with a jam or strawberry sauces.


This version of the recipe in particular is an old family recipe that has been passed down for generations. The original recipe card it is on has many notes and modifications over the years, as well as stains and old hand writing that can make it hard to read.

Instructions:



1. Preheat oven to 300°F. Spray an 8x8 or 9x9 baking pan with cooking spray.


2. In a standmixer or in a larger bowl, cream the butter until fluffy. Add 1 cup of sugar gradually and mix until fluffy. Add flour and knead until a dough forms. If you have a crumbly dough, keep mixing until it comes together.


3. Press the dough into the pan and use a fork to poke holes in the dough. Sprinkle the remaining 2 tbsp of sugar on top.


4. Cook at 300°F for 30 minutes, or until golden brown edges. Let cool for 15-20 minutes before removing from the pan.
